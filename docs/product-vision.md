# EV Compare — Product Vision

## 1. Vision

EV Compare aims to make electric vehicle selection accessible, informed and engaging for everyone, regardless of their initial technical knowledge.

The platform helps people identify and compare electric vehicles based on their actual needs, while providing opportunities to progressively explore and understand the technologies behind them.

**Complexity should be available, never mandatory.**

EV Compare does not aim to eliminate technical complexity, but to make it understandable and explorable, allowing users to decide independently how deeply they want to engage with it.

## 2. Target Audience

EV Compare serves three overlapping audiences:

**Prospective EV buyers**

People considering an electric vehicle who primarily want to identify suitable options based on their budget, habits, constraints and preferences, without needing prior technical knowledge.

**Curious and learning users**

People who want to understand why certain vehicles perform differently and how technical characteristics affect everyday ownership and driving experiences.

**EV enthusiasts**

Technically knowledgeable users looking for accessible yet sufficiently detailed tools to analyze vehicles, explore their characteristics and compare technical performance.

These are not separate user categories or fixed levels of expertise. A person may move naturally between them depending on their goals and interests.

## 3. Core Product Principles

### 3.1 Direct access, without unnecessary guidance

Users must be able to immediately search, filter, explore and compare vehicles.

The platform must not require introductory questionnaires, tutorials, knowledge assessments or guided onboarding before granting access to its core functionality.

Assistance should be available when useful, not imposed.

### 3.2 Needs before technical specifications

Users should be able to identify suitable vehicles through everyday requirements, such as travel habits, charging availability, budget and practical constraints.

Technical knowledge must not be a prerequisite for expressing these needs.

At the same time, users who already understand EV specifications must be able to work directly with technical filters and parameters.

### 3.3 Progressive depth

Every important feature should offer an accessible starting point and, where meaningful, opportunities for deeper exploration.

Information should progress naturally from practical meaning to technical explanation and detailed analysis.

Users must remain in control of how much complexity they engage with.

### 3.4 Learning through interaction

EV Compare should favor experiential learning over passive consumption of educational content.

Interactive comparisons, simulations, visualizations and contextual explanations should help users discover how EV technologies work and how they affect real-world decisions.

Learning must emerge naturally from using the product, rather than being presented as a separate mandatory activity.

### 3.5 Technical depth without unnecessary complexity

EV Compare should provide meaningful technical analysis tools for knowledgeable users and enthusiasts.

These tools should enable exploration of charging behavior, energy consumption, range and other relevant characteristics without attempting to replicate the full analytical depth of highly specialized engineering platforms.

Technical rigor and usability should reinforce each other.

### 3.6 Transparent recommendations

Vehicle recommendations and comparisons must be understandable and traceable to the user's expressed needs and the underlying available data.

The platform should explain why a vehicle may be suitable, which factors influenced a result, and where data limitations or model assumptions apply.

Estimates must be distinguishable from measured or verified information.

### 3.7 Respect for user autonomy

EV Compare should treat users as capable decision-makers, regardless of their experience.

It should avoid patronizing language, unnecessary simplifications, artificial knowledge levels and forced educational paths.

Its purpose is to support informed decisions, not to make them on the user's behalf.

## 4. Desired User Experience

A user entering EV Compare should immediately recognize that they can search for and compare electric vehicles.

The primary interface should expose meaningful search and filtering capabilities, without requiring preliminary interaction.

From there, users should be able to:

1. Identify vehicles compatible with their needs.
2. Understand the practical reasons behind differences and recommendations.
3. Explore relevant technical concepts directly through interactive tools.
4. Access progressively more detailed information and analytical capabilities.
5. Return to their original comparison without losing context.

The interface should accommodate both quick, practical decisions and extended technical exploration.

**An inexperienced user should never feel overwhelmed by what they do not know. An experienced user should never feel constrained by what the interface assumes they do not know.**

## 5. Visual and Interaction Identity

EV Compare should feel like an accessible, modern environment for automotive exploration and technical discovery.

Its design should balance:

- **Clarity:** information organized around user needs and meaningful outcomes.
- **Personality:** a distinctive visual language rather than a generic administrative dashboard.
- **Explorability:** interfaces that encourage experimentation and discovery.
- **Precision:** technical information presented accurately and consistently.
- **Continuity:** smooth transitions between basic comparisons and advanced analysis.

The visual identity should support the product philosophy without relying on unnecessary decoration, excessive animations or forced gamification.

## 6. What EV Compare Is Not

EV Compare is not:

- A mandatory guided questionnaire for choosing an electric vehicle.
- A traditional automotive catalogue focused primarily on specifications.
- An educational website built around extensive collections of articles.
- A technical dashboard designed exclusively for EV enthusiasts.
- An engineering-grade vehicle simulation platform.
- A platform that hides uncertainty or oversimplifies technical information to make recommendations appear definitive.

## 7. Product Promise

EV Compare enables people to find the electric vehicles that suit them, understand what makes those vehicles different, and explore the technology behind their choices — at their own pace and desired level of depth.

**Find what fits. Understand why. Explore further.**
